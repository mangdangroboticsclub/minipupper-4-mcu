RPM test

5° → 305° → 5° at full power. The robot times how long the servo takes from 35° to 275°: RPM = 240° ÷ time ÷ 6. Leg off / horn free.







  
Spin test (like Dynamixel “no load speed”)

  
Spins the servo round and round at a fixed current, waits 1 s, then counts turns: RPM = turns ÷ minutes. Up, then down. Horn free / leg off, and only a servo that turns 360° by hand — if it does not turn, it stops by itself after 2 s.

  

    
    
    
  

  

  






  
Torque test (position mode)

  
Reads where the servo is, moves it move° from there (up if it fits in 0–310°, else down) with the current below, holds it, goes back gently (300 mA), then goes limp so you can turn the horn by hand.
